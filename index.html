01 / Who we are

A place to belong.
A faith to live.

People’s Place Church exists to establish a Kingdom-centered community where people discover their true place.

We want to see people walk in the freedom, love, and power of Christ in every part of life. Through worship, teaching, discipleship, and community, we seek to draw closer to God and carry His presence into the world around us.

If you’ve wrestled with doubt, felt disillusioned, or struggled to find your place in church, we want to make room for you.

Read our full mission & vision +

People’s Place Church exists to establish a Kingdom-centered community where people discover their true place—not just in a building, but in the presence of God, in their daily lives, and in the world around them.

We envision a church that serves as a hub of faith and transformation, with a central body and a network of affiliated churches working together as part of a unified Kingdom family. Through both physical gatherings and digital outreach, we will create spaces for worship, teaching, and discipleship that draw people into deeper faith.

We are committed to reaching those who have struggled to find their place in the Church—those wrestling with doubt, disillusionment, or feeling like they don’t quite fit in traditional faith spaces. We will confront real struggles—the problem of suffering, hypocrisy in leadership, and the disconnect between faith on Sunday and faith in everyday life.

Ultimately, we desire to see people walk in full Kingdom citizenship—not just experiencing God’s presence momentarily, but carrying it into every aspect of their lives. We believe the Church should equip believers to live in the freedom, love, and power of Christ, not just on Sundays, but in every moment.

03 / Our foundation

What shapes
our faith.

Explore the beliefs at the heart of People’s Place, with Scripture to read and reflect on.

01No King but Jesus+

We are first and foremost citizens of the Kingdom of God. While we recognize earthly responsibilities and authorities, our ultimate allegiance is to Christ alone. We view the world through the lens of His rule and reign, and we will never submit to any ideology, culture, or movement that contradicts His Kingdom.

Philippians 3:20Colossians 1:13–14Matthew 6:33John 18:36
02Deny Him Nothing+

We believe in full surrender to the Holy Spirit, even when it leads us beyond our comfort zone. The Church should never stifle the Spirit’s movement simply because it feels unfamiliar. While we hold fast to biblical truth, we will always make room for God to move in new and unexpected ways, ensuring we remain a people who seek revelation, not restriction.

Romans 12:12 Corinthians 3:171 Thessalonians 5:19–21Isaiah 55:8–9
03We Relish the Risk+

Faith requires risk. We are called to believe for the impossible, not with a safety net of doubt, but with bold expectation. Whether we are praying for healing, provision, or breakthrough, we refuse to limit God’s power by allowing human logic or fear to dictate our faith.

Hebrews 11:6Matthew 17:20Mark 16:17–18James 1:6
04The All-or-Nothing Gospel+

The Bible is either completely true, or none of it is. We stand on the full authority of Scripture as God’s unshakable Word, not a collection of good ideas. We will live and teach a faith that does not pick and choose what is convenient, but embraces the whole truth of God’s Word in both grace and obedience.

2 Timothy 3:16–17Matthew 4:4Hebrews 4:12John 17:17
05The Presence of God is Our Priority+

The Church is not just a place for learning about God—it’s a place for experiencing Him. We believe that encountering His presence is the key to transformation, healing, and purpose. Whether in worship, prayer, or daily life, we seek to be a people who dwell in His presence, not just visit it on Sundays.

Exodus 33:14–15Psalm 16:11Proverbs 3:5–6John 15:4–5
06The Church is a People, Not a Place+

The Kingdom of God is not confined to a building. We believe ministry happens everywhere—in workplaces, homes, coffee shops, and creative spaces. Church is not an event we attend, but an identity we carry, influencing the world around us every day.

Acts 17:241 Corinthians 3:16Matthew 18:20Ephesians 2:19–22
07Grace and Righteousness Go Hand in Hand+

We are saved by grace, but we are also called to live holy, set-apart lives. We reject both legalism (which demands righteousness without grace) and cheap grace (which excuses sin without transformation). Instead, we live in the tension of 100% grace and 100% righteousness, just as Jesus demonstrated.

Romans 6:1–2Ephesians 2:8–10Titus 2:11–12James 2:17
